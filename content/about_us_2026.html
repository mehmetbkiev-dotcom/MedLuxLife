Treatment in Türkiye.
Travel wherever you wish.

MedLuxLife was founded by Anna and Emrah in Aachen, Germany. We bring two things together: medical treatment with qualified specialists at our partner clinics and hospitals in Türkiye – and travel to any destination you choose, whether a holiday, a city break, a business trip or a pilgrimage.

We adapt to you – to your wishes, your budget and your pace. No wish is too big or too small: we always look for the comfortable solution that suits you, and one personal contact plans every step with you.

What we do

Health

We are your coordinator, not a clinic. We listen to your wishes, arrange your consultation with qualified specialists at our partner clinics and hospitals in Türkiye and organise your stay. All medical decisions are made by licensed doctors, together with you.

Travel

Travel is not limited to Türkiye: we plan holidays, city breaks, business trips and pilgrimages to the destination of your choice – flights, hand-picked hotels, private transfers and excursions, tailored to your wishes and budget.
