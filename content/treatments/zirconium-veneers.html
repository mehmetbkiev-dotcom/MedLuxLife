Zirconium veneers and crowns are custom-made, metal-free restorations in zirconia ceramic. They cover discolouration, chips or gaps and improve the shape and colour of your teeth with a natural, light-transmitting look. Who it may be suitable for

  • Your teeth are stained, chipped, slightly misaligned or have gaps.
  • You would like a durable, natural-looking solution – also suitable if you have a metal allergy.
  • Your gums are healthy and there is enough enamel for veneers.

Whether the treatment is suitable for you is decided by the dentist after a personal assessment.

How the treatment works

Consultation and planning

The dentist assesses your teeth and plans the treatment – often with digital images that show the planned result.

Tooth preparation

A thin layer of enamel is removed under local anaesthesia and precise digital impressions are taken.

Veneer fabrication

The veneers are made in a dental laboratory to match the shape, colour and size you planned.

Bonding and final adjustments

After a trial fitting, the veneers are bonded and fine-tuned for comfort and a natural look.
