Breast augmentation increases the size and volume of the breasts or restores volume lost after weight changes or pregnancy – with implants or with your own fat. Implant type, size and incision site are chosen with your surgeon. MedLuxLife arranges your online consultation with the surgeon in Istanbul and organises your stay.

Who it may be suitable for

  • You would like more breast volume or a different shape.
  • You would like to restore volume after weight changes or pregnancy.
  • Your breasts are noticeably uneven.
  • Your weight has been stable for some time.
  • You are in good general health.

Whether the procedure is suitable for you is decided by the surgeon after a personal assessment.

What can be chosen

Silicone or saline implants

Different shapes and sizes, chosen to suit your body.

Your own fat

Fat transfer: your own fat is moved to the breasts.

Incision site

Under the breast, around the areola or in the armpit.

How the operation is performed

Preparation and anaesthesia

After a detailed consultation about your wishes and your health, the operation takes place under general anaesthesia.

Incision and implant placement

A small incision is made under the breast, around the areola or in the armpit, and the implant is placed for the planned size and shape.

Shaping and symmetry

The surgeon shapes the breasts and adjusts the position of the implants – or the distribution of transferred fat – to your proportions.

Closure and recovery

The incisions are closed with fine sutures and you are monitored in the recovery area. You usually stay in hospital for one night.

Recovery step by step

First days

Walking is possible within hours. A supportive bra helps healing and reduces swelling.

Week 1

Most people return to work and light routines after about 5–7 days.

Weeks 4–6

The supportive bra is worn for about 4–6 weeks; sport and heavy lifting come later, as advised.
