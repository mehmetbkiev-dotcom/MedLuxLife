General information; your dentist plans the details for you personally.

Who it may be suitable for

  • Your teeth are stained or discoloured – for example from coffee, tea, red wine, smoking, age or certain medication.
  • Your teeth and gums are healthy.
  • You know that the achievable shade differs from person to person.

Whether the treatment is suitable for you is decided by the dentist after a personal assessment.

Options

In-clinic whitening

A professional gel activated by LED or laser light.

Take-home kit

Custom trays for gradual whitening at home.

How the treatment works

Consultation and cleaning

The dentist checks your teeth and gums, and the teeth are cleaned to prepare them for whitening.

Whitening gel

A professional whitening gel is applied, often activated with a special light.

Monitoring and sessions

The process is monitored for an even result; depending on the desired shade, more sessions may be recommended.

Aftercare

Avoid colouring food and drinks for 48 hours; touch-up kits help to keep the result.
