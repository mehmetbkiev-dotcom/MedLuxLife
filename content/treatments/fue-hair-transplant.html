In an FUE hair transplant, individual hair follicles are taken one by one from the donor area – usually the back or sides of the head – with a fine micro-punch and implanted into thinning or bald areas. No linear incision and no stitches are needed. MedLuxLife arranges your online consultation with the surgeon in Istanbul and organises your stay.

Who it may be suitable for

  • You have male pattern baldness, thinning hair or a receding hairline.
  • You have enough healthy donor hair at the back or sides of the head.
  • Your hair loss has stabilised.
  • You are in good general health.

Whether the procedure is suitable for you is decided by the surgeon after a personal assessment.

How the procedure works

Preparation and anaesthesia

The surgeon discusses your wishes, examines your hair and scalp and plans the hairline. Local anaesthesia is applied on the day of the procedure.

Follicle extraction

With fine micro-punches, individual follicles are taken from the donor area at the back or sides of the head, where hair is most resistant to hair loss.

Implantation

The follicles are implanted into the thinning or bald areas, following your natural direction of growth.

Closure and recovery

FUE needs no stitches. You receive personal aftercare instructions, for example on how to wash your hair.

Recovery step by step

First days

Light activities after 1–2 days; most people return to work after about 2–3 days. Strenuous exercise should wait at least one month.

Weeks 2–4

The transplanted hairs fall out temporarily ("shock loss") – a normal part of healing; the roots stay in place.

Months 3–18

New hair grows from about month 3, density increases noticeably by month 6, and the final result shows after 12–18 months.
