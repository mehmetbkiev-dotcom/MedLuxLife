In a Brazilian Butt Lift, fat is removed by liposuction from areas such as the abdomen or flanks, purified and transferred to the buttocks for more volume and shape. MedLuxLife arranges your online consultation with the surgeon in Istanbul and organises your stay.

Who it may be suitable for

  • You would like more volume and a different shape of the buttocks.
  • You have enough body fat in other areas for the transfer.
  • Your weight has been stable for some time.
  • You are in good general health.

Whether the procedure is suitable for you is decided by the surgeon after a personal assessment.

How the operation is performed

Consultation with the surgeon

Before the operation, the surgeon examines you in person, discusses your wishes and confirms the final plan.

Anaesthesia and preparation

On the day of surgery you receive general anaesthesia and sleep throughout the operation. The team marks and prepares the area to be treated.

The procedure

Fat is removed by liposuction, purified and injected into the buttocks according to the plan.

Closure and first care

The small incisions are closed and a compression garment is put on. You usually stay in hospital for 1–2 nights.

Recovery step by step

Weeks 1–3

Avoid sitting directly on your buttocks for 2–3 weeks; a special pillow helps when sitting is necessary.

Weeks 1–6

The compression garment is worn for about 6 weeks to reduce swelling and support the new contour.

Months 1–3

Part of the transferred fat is naturally absorbed by the body in the first months; the rest stays.
