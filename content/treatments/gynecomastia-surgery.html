Who it may be suitable for

  • You have enlarged breast tissue, for example due to hormones, genetics, weight changes or medication.
  • The cause has been checked by a doctor.
  • Your weight has been stable for some time.
  • You are in good general health.

Whether the procedure is suitable for you is decided by the surgeon after a personal assessment.

Techniques

Liposuction

Removes excess fat through small incisions.

Tissue removal

Removes firm glandular tissue directly.

Combination

Both techniques together, if needed.

How the operation is performed

Preparation and anaesthesia

After a detailed consultation about your wishes and your health, the operation takes place under general or local anaesthesia.

Liposuction and tissue removal

Excess fat is removed by liposuction; where needed, glandular tissue and excess skin are removed directly.

Contouring the chest

If a lot of tissue is removed, the nipples may be repositioned. The chest is shaped evenly on both sides.

Closure and recovery

The incisions are closed and a compression vest is put on. You go home the same day or after one night.

Weeks 3–4

Later
