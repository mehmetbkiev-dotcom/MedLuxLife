A dental implant is a fixed replacement for a missing tooth: a titanium post is placed in the jawbone as an artificial root and, once it has grown into the bone, a custom-made crown is attached. Implants restore chewing function and help to preserve the jawbone. MedLuxLife arranges your online consultation with the dentist in Istanbul and organises your stay.

Who it may be suitable for

  • You are missing one or more teeth and would like a fixed replacement.
  • Your gums are healthy and there is enough jawbone – if not, bone grafting or a sinus lift can help.
  • You are in good general health and ready to keep up good oral hygiene.

Whether the treatment is suitable for you is decided by the dentist after a personal assessment.

Options

Single implant

Replaces one tooth with an implant and a crown.

Several implants

Implant-supported crowns or bridges replace several teeth.

Bone grafting or sinus lift

Builds up the bone if there is not enough for an implant.

How the treatment works

Preparation and anaesthesia

The dentist discusses your wishes and examines your teeth and jaw. The implant is placed under local anaesthesia.

Implant placement

A titanium post is placed precisely into the jawbone as an artificial tooth root.

Healing (osseointegration)

Over the next 3–6 months, the implant grows firmly into the jawbone – you return home in the meantime.

Crown placement

On your second visit, the custom-made crown is attached to the implant.

Healing step by step

First days

Mild swelling and discomfort after the numbness wears off are treated with mild painkillers; most people resume light activities the next day.

Months 3–6

The implant grows firmly into the jawbone (osseointegration). You are at home during this time.

Second visit

The permanent crown is fitted – about 5–7 days in Türkiye.
