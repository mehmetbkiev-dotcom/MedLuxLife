A beard or moustache transplant gives a fuller, more defined beard or moustache. Follicles, usually from the back of the head, are transplanted into sparse or hairless areas of the face with the FUE or DHI technique, following the natural direction of growth. Who it may be suitable for

  • Your beard or moustache grows patchy, thin or unevenly.
  • You have lost facial hair due to genetics, scars or an injury.
  • You have enough healthy donor hair.
  • You are in good general health.

Whether the procedure is suitable for you is decided by the surgeon after a personal assessment.

Options

Beard transplant

For cheeks, jawline and chin – density and design are planned with you.

Moustache transplant

For the upper lip, symmetrical and in the natural direction of growth.

How the procedure works

Preparation and anaesthesia

After a detailed consultation about your wishes and the design, the procedure takes place under local anaesthesia.

Follicle harvesting

Follicles are taken from the donor area at the back of the head with the FUE or DHI technique.

Implantation and design

The follicles are implanted into the beard or moustache area in the natural direction of growth; density and shape are planned with you.

Closure and recovery

The donor area is cared for and you receive detailed aftercare instructions.

Recovery step by step

First week

Redness and slight swelling go down; most people are back to their routines within a week.

Weeks 2–4

The transplanted hairs fall out temporarily ("shock loss"); the roots stay in place.

Months 3–12

New growth starts around month 3, visible density at about 6 months, and the final result after 9–12 months.
