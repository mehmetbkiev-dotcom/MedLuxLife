Liposuction removes stubborn fat deposits that do not respond to diet and exercise, for example on the abdomen, waist, thighs, arms or neck, to shape the body contour. It is not a method for losing weight. MedLuxLife arranges your online consultation with the surgeon in Istanbul and organises your stay.

Who it may be suitable for

  • You have local fat deposits that do not respond to diet and exercise.
  • Your weight is stable and close to your goal weight.
  • You are in good general health.
  • You understand that liposuction shapes the body but is not a weight-loss treatment.

Whether the procedure is suitable for you is decided by the surgeon after a personal assessment.

How the operation is performed

Consultation with the surgeon

Before the operation, the surgeon examines you in person, discusses your wishes and confirms the final plan.

Anaesthesia and preparation

The type of anaesthesia – local with sedation or general anaesthesia – is planned with you in advance. The team marks and prepares the area to be treated.

The procedure

Through small incisions, the surgeon removes the fat with thin cannulas – depending on the plan also with Vaser or laser-assisted techniques – and shapes the treated areas.

Closure and first care

The small incisions are closed and a compression garment is put on. Depending on the extent, you go home the same day or after one night.

Recovery step by step

First days

Walking is possible within hours. The compression garment reduces swelling.

Weeks 3–6

First changes in shape usually show after 3–4 weeks. The garment is worn for 4–6 weeks.

Months 3–6

Once the swelling has gone, the final result usually shows after 3–6 months.
