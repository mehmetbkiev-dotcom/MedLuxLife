Rhinoplasty reshapes the bone and cartilage of the nose – to change its size or shape, to correct an asymmetry or, where needed, to improve breathing. The plan is made together with your surgeon. MedLuxLife arranges your online consultation with the surgeon in Istanbul and organises your stay.

Who it may be suitable for

  • You would like to change the shape or size of your nose.
  • You have breathing problems, for example because of a deviated septum.
  • The growth of your face is complete.
  • You are in good general health and do not smoke, or are ready to stop for the surgery.

Whether the procedure is suitable for you is decided by the surgeon after a personal assessment.

Types of rhinoplasty

Cosmetic rhinoplasty

Changes the size, tip or nostrils, or corrects an asymmetry.

Functional rhinoplasty

Septorhinoplasty improves breathing, for example with a deviated septum.

How the operation is performed

Consultation before surgery

The surgeon examines your facial structure, discusses your wishes and makes a personal surgical plan.

Anaesthesia and preparation

You receive general anaesthesia or local anaesthesia with sedation. The area is prepared and precise markings guide the reshaping.

Reshaping the nose

Through small incisions inside the nostrils or at the base of the nose, the surgeon reshapes bone and cartilage – shape, size and symmetry – and corrects breathing problems where needed.

Closure and recovery

The incisions are closed carefully and a splint supports the new shape. You are monitored closely and receive a personal aftercare plan.

Recovery step by step

First week

Bruising and swelling around the eyes usually go down within 7–10 days. The splint is removed after 5–7 days.

First month

Most of the swelling goes down. Avoid strenuous activity and follow the care instructions of your surgeon.

Months 6–12

The tissues settle slowly; the final shape of the nose usually shows after 6–12 months.
