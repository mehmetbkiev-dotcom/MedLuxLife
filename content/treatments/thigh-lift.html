Who it may be suitable for

  • You have loose skin and excess fat on your thighs, for example after major weight loss or with age.
  • Your weight has been stable for some time.
  • You are in good general health.

Whether the procedure is suitable for you is decided by the surgeon after a personal assessment.

Options

Inner thigh lift

Incisions along the inner thighs for loose skin on the inside of the legs.

Outer thigh lift

Incisions along the outer thighs to tighten the outer contour.

How the operation is performed

Consultation before surgery

The surgeon assesses the areas of concern, discusses your wishes and makes a personal surgical plan.

Anaesthesia and preparation

You receive general anaesthesia. The area is marked to guide the incisions.

Removing excess skin and fat

Incisions are made along the inner or outer thighs; excess skin and fat are removed and the tissue underneath is tightened.

Closure and recovery

The incisions are closed with sutures and compression garments are put on. You usually stay in hospital for 1–2 nights.

Recovery step by step

First days

Walking is possible within hours, and light movement helps circulation. You usually stay in hospital for 1–2 nights.

First weeks

Compression garments reduce swelling and support healing; strenuous activity should be avoided.

Later

Sport and heavy exertion come back step by step, as advised by your surgeon.
