# MedLuxLife – Arabic (ar) translation rules

Source: British English. Each string is one text node (or alt/placeholder/aria-label/title/button text) of a live page; translate ONLY the given strings. The Arabic pages are shown right-to-left (dir="rtl").

## Style
- Modern Standard Arabic (الفصحى المعاصرة), natural and fluent, like a premium clinic / travel agency for readers in the Gulf and the wider Arab world. Calm, warm, trustworthy tone. Translate the meaning, not word for word.
- Address the reader in the polite plural, consistently: «أنتم / ـكم», imperatives «تواصلوا معنا»، «احجزوا»، «اكتشفوا». Short menu items and buttons may use the common fixed forms in the tables below.
- Arabic punctuation: comma «،», semicolon «؛», question mark «؟». Quotes «…». Sentence dashes « – ».
- Medical advertising law (German HWG – applies to all languages): no promises or guarantees, no superlatives that are not in the source, no added "painless / risk-free / permanent / best". Keep cautious words ("usually" = «عادةً», "may" = «قد»).
- Keep facts exactly: numbers, ranges, units, ages, percentages. Use Western digits (0–9) exactly as in the source (no Arabic-Indic digits). Ranges keep the en dash ("2–4").
- Keep unchanged in Latin letters: MedLuxLife, WhatsApp, Instagram, Facebook, e-mail addresses, phone numbers, URLs, technique/brand names (FUE, DHI, PRP, NAD+, Choi, Vaser, SMAS, All-on-4, All-on-6, BBL, IVF, TOETVA, LASIK …). Medical abbreviations may stay Latin (MRI, CT) or be written «الرنين المغناطيسي (MRI)».
- Arrows point the other way in Arabic: "→" becomes "←" and "←" becomes "→" (keep it at the same logical place: "Learn more →" = «اعرف المزيد ←», "← Back" = «→ رجوع»). The sign ">" (written &gt;) stays exactly as it is (the browser mirrors it).
- Keep HTML entities that stand for symbols (&amp; may become «و» inside page names; &gt; stays). Do not add HTML tags. Numbering at the start ("1.") stays.
- Leave a string exactly as it is (return the same text) when it is: German (legal texts, "Impressum", "Datenschutz", "AGB", "Widerrufsbelehrung", "Stornierungsbedingungen", German consents, "gelesen."), a language name written in its own language ("English", "Deutsch", "Türkçe", "Українська", "Русский", "Français", "Español", "Italiano", "العربية" …), a brand, a phone number or e-mail.
- Never name the partner clinic, hospital or doctors. "our partner hospital" = «المستشفى الشريك لنا», "our partner clinic(s)" = «العيادة الشريكة لنا» / «عياداتنا الشريكة».
- Medical documents: never ask people to send documents to MedLuxLife; if the English says documents go directly to the doctor, keep exactly that meaning.
- Places: Türkiye = «تركيا», Istanbul = «إسطنبول», Germany = «ألمانيا», Aachen = «آخن», Antalya = «أنطاليا», Cappadocia = «كبادوكيا», Mecca = «مكة المكرمة», Medina = «المدينة المنورة».
- Hajj and Umrah texts: respectful, standard Islamic terms (الحج، العمرة، المناسك، الإحرام، الميقات، الطواف، السعي).
- Gender: for patients use neutral/plural wording where possible («المرضى»، «الضيوف»). Captions (alt texts) describe the picture neutrally.
