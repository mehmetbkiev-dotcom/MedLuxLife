A mommy makeover combines several procedures in one operation to address changes after pregnancy and childbirth – for example loose skin and separated abdominal muscles, a change in breast shape or stubborn fat deposits. Which procedures are combined is decided together with your surgeon. MedLuxLife arranges your online consultation with the surgeon in Istanbul and organises your stay.

Who it may be suitable for

  • You have completed your family planning and are no longer breastfeeding.
  • Your weight has been stable for some time.
  • You are in good general health.
  • You would like to address several changes after pregnancy at the same time.

Whether the procedure is suitable for you is decided by the surgeon after a personal assessment.

What it can include

Tummy tuck

Removes excess skin and tightens the abdominal muscles.

Breast lift or augmentation

Restores the shape and volume of the breasts.

Liposuction

Removes stubborn fat on the abdomen, hips or thighs.

Intimate rejuvenation

For example labiaplasty, if you wish.

How the operation is performed

Consultation with the surgeon

Before the operation, the surgeon examines you in person, discusses your wishes and confirms the final plan – which procedures are combined.

Anaesthesia and preparation

On the day of surgery you receive general anaesthesia and sleep throughout the operation. The team marks and prepares the areas to be treated.

The combined procedures

The planned procedures – for example a tummy tuck, breast surgery and liposuction – are performed in one session. The duration depends on the combination.

Closure and first care

The incisions are closed carefully and dressed. You are closely monitored in the first hours and usually stay in hospital for 1–2 nights.

Recovery step by step

First days

Gentle movement soon after surgery and 1–2 nights in hospital. Follow the care instructions of your surgeon.

Weeks 2–4

Most people gradually return to their daily activities. Heavy lifting and sport come later, as advised.

Months 6–12

Swelling goes down over the weeks; the final result usually shows after 6–12 months.
